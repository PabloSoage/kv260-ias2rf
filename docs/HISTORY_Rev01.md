# History of the Rev01 work-in-progress

*Record of what was done between the Altium import and August 2026, kept for traceability. The
assessment of each phase reflects the later audit; see [AUDIT_Rev01.md](AUDIT_Rev01.md).*

---

## Starting point

A KiCad project imported from Altium (`CSI2-KRIA-KV260_Rev01`): the Allied Vision MIPI camera
adapter board, with two Hirose BM20B(0.8)-34DP connectors to the Kria KV260, MIPI FFC/FPC
connectors, regulators, I2C/GPIO level shifters and USB power.

---

## Phase A — Purge of the original schematic

**Goal:** remove the MIPI camera electronics while keeping the Hirose connectors and the Kria power
rails.

| Action | Detail |
|---|---|
| Direct deletion (15 blocks) | J1 (USB-B), J7 (MIPI FFC22), J2/J5/J6/J9/J10 (headers), U1 (INA260), U2 (TPS25200), U3 (PCA9306), U4 (NTB0104), U5 (NCP114), D1 (ESD), D2/D3 (LEDs) |
| Cascade deletion (34 R/C) | 4 ERC iterations → 24+7+2+1 orphaned passives |
| Wire cleanup | 133 dangling wires and 22 junctions, located by coordinates from the ERC report |
| Kept | J8 (Hirose ×2), X1, TP1-6, MRK1-6, PCB1, LA1, T1/T2, 66 power symbols |

**Metrics:** 134 → 85 components · 29,298 → ~19,430 lines · ERC 774 → 396

**Assessment: solid, usable work.** It is the base for Rev03.

**Side effect not noticed at the time:** deleting `J1` (USB-B) removed the origin of the
`VCC5V-EXT` rail `[V — J1_IAS1 is the only source of that net in the .kicad_pcb]`, which was left
orphaned. The Rev01 report went on listing it afterwards as if it came from the Hirose connectors.

## Phase B — Pinout extraction

Pinouts for 15 chips extracted from an EasyEDA library and documented in `_work/pinouts.txt`
(556 lines). Created `sym-lib-table` and `fp-lib-table` pointing at `${KICAD_3RD_PARTY}/EasyEDA`.

**Assessment: real, reusable work** for whichever components survive the BOM review.

---

## Phase C — `FRONTEND.kicad_sch` generated by script

Generated with `_work/phaseC_gen_frontend.py`: 14 chip instances with inlined `lib_symbols`,
60 hierarchical labels, a power tree, a clock chain, ADC, DAC, mixer, 3 switches, an RPi header,
4 SMAs, 16 decoupling capacitors, 38 no-connects and 17 visual wires.

**Assessment: discarded.** Not because of the technique, but because of the content:

- The ADL5801 mixer does not reach the 10.5 GHz it was specified for.
- The AD9767's AVDD was left on the 1.8 V rail, on a 3.3 V device.
- The 1.8 V LDO (ADP151, 200 mA) is undersized and dissipates 1.6 W in a TSOT-23-5.
- Anti-alias filter, reconstruction filter, LNA, baluns and synthesiser are missing entirely.
- The EasyEDA symbols carry no electrical types: 48 `pin_to_pin` conflicts ERC cannot interpret.

Moved to `_deprecated/` and removed from the project on 21 September 2026.

---

## Phase D — Hierarchical synchronisation and netclasses

54 local labels converted to `hierarchical_label`; 13 sheet pins added; "50 Ohm" and "100 Ohm"
netclasses with patterns `ANT_*`, `MIX_*`, `RF_SW*`, `CLK_*`, `B7-CSI2-*`.

**Assessment: partly usable.** The mechanism is correct. The netclasses are decorative until a
stackup exists: 4 layers all declared `signal` gives no impedance control.

---

## Phase E — `PHASED_ARRAY.kicad_sch` generated by script

8× ADAR1000 with an SPI daisy-chain, `ADDR0`/`ADDR1` strapped, common `TR_CTRL` and `TX_LOAD`,
8 `RF_ANT_1..8` labels, 1 SMA and — according to the report — an 8-pin interface header.

**Assessment: discarded.**

- The B units of all 8 ADAR1000s, which hold the power pins, **were not placed**: the chips had no
  power `[V — 8 `missing_power_pin` errors]`.
- The interface header **was not instantiated** `[V — the only symbols were 8 ADAR1000s and 1 SMA]`.
- Zero decoupling capacitors.
- `ADDR0`/`ADDR1` give 4 addresses, not the 8 the report described; and addressing and daisy-chain
  are alternatives, not cumulative.
- Each ADAR1000 has 4 channels: 8 chips are 32 elements, not 8.

**And above all:** this block does not need designing. The open
[AERIS-10 / PLFM_RADAR](https://github.com/NawfalMotii79/PLFM_RADAR) project already has the
ADAR1000s, the T/R front ends, the mixers, the synthesisers and the array solved.

Moved to `_deprecated/` and removed from the project on 21 September 2026.

---

## Phase F — "PCB placement"

34 footprints inserted into the `.kicad_pcb` and power symbols annotated.

**Assessment: it did not exist as placement.**

- The 34 footprints were **shells with not a single pad** `[V]`.
- They sat **outside the board outline**, at Y = 145…245 mm against an outline ending at
  Y = 127.3 mm `[V]`.
- References did not match the schematic (`U_ADC` against `U_ADC1`, and so on) `[V]`.
- The `0 unconnected pads` reported as proof of correct connectivity was zero **because there were
  no pads**.

Removed on 14 August 2026; backup in `_backup_original/`.

## About the scripts in `_work/`

They hold both valid work and work not to repeat:

| Script | Reusable |
|---|---|
| `phaseA1_purge.py`, `phaseA2_cleanup_wires.py`, `phaseA3_iterative.py` | **Yes** — purge and cleanup, well done |
| `phaseB_extract_pins.py`, `inventory.py` | **Yes** — data extraction |
| `debug_*.py`, `fix_*.py` | One-off utilities |
| `rev03_drop_dead_sheets.py` | **Yes** — removes the dead sheets from the project, with verification |
| `phaseC_*.py`, `phaseD_final.py`, `phaseE_phased_array.py`, `phaseF_placement.py` | **No** — schematic generation and placement by script |

The lesson is in the [method note of the audit](AUDIT_Rev01.md#8-method-note): generating
schematics by script and validating them by counting ERC violations does not work when the symbols
have no electrical types and the circuit is RF. A falling ERC count does not mean the circuit is
improving; it means there is less that ERC knows how to check.
