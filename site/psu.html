PSU board

Feeds the adapter and the KV260 from the car's battery or a 6 to 36 V bench supply, each by its own cable from one ground.

  • LTC4364 surge stopper: Load Dump A clamped at 48 V, reverse input to -40 V
  • LM5177 four-switch buck-boost to 12.0 V, 2.9 to 60 V in
  • TPS26631 eFuses: 2.0 A to the adapter (J2), 3.0 A to the KV260 (J3)
  • 110 x 70 mm, 4 layers; ERC 0, DRC 0 / 0
